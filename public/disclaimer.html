Affiliate disclosure

DealMint participates in affiliate programmes operated by Amazon, Flipkart and affiliate networks. Some links on this site are affiliate links, which means that if you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission from the seller.

This costs you nothing. You pay exactly the price the seller is charging; the commission comes out of the seller's margin, not your pocket.

Commission does not decide what appears here. Deals are selected by discount threshold and ordered by when they were found, using data read from the seller's own listing. We do not accept payment for placement, and we do not rank a deal higher because it pays more.

Pricing disclaimer

Prices and discounts shown on DealMint are captured automatically at the time of crawling and are not live. Between that moment and the moment you click, a price can rise, an offer can expire, or stock can run out.

The final price also depends on factors we cannot see, including:

  • your delivery location and applicable taxes
  • the specific seller fulfilling the item
  • your account type and any membership benefits
  • coupons, bank offers and exchange discounts applied at checkout
  • shipping charges

Always verify the price, seller and delivery terms on the marketplace before completing a purchase. The seller's page prevails over anything shown here.

Discount and "lowest price" labels

Discount percentages are those published by the seller, calculated against the maximum retail price they state. We do not independently verify that an MRP reflects a price the product genuinely sold at.

Where we mark a deal as a price drop or as the lowest we have seen, that statement is limited to prices we have observed since we began tracking that product. It is not a claim about the product's entire price history.
