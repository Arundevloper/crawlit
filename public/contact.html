Report a bug or a wrong deal

The prices here are read automatically from seller pages, so things occasionally go wrong: a stale price, a dead link, a product in the wrong category, or a listing that should not be on the site at all. Telling us is the fastest way to get it fixed.

What to include

  • The deal's title, or a link to the DealMint page it appeared on
  • What looked wrong, and what you expected instead
  • Your device and browser, if the problem is with how the page displays
  • A screenshot, if you have one — it usually saves a round of questions
