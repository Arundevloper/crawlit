Privacy Policy

The short version: there are no accounts on DealMint, and we do not build a profile of you.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

In one line: we do not ask you to sign up, we do not run advertising trackers, and we do not sell data. What follows is the detail.

Information we do not collect

DealMint has no user accounts, no login, and no newsletter signup. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, postal address or payment details, because nothing on the site requires them.

We do not operate advertising networks or cross-site tracking pixels, and we do not sell or rent information about you to anyone.

Information handled automatically

Server logs

Like virtually every website, our server records standard technical request data — IP address, browser user-agent, the page requested and the time. This is used to keep the service running and to investigate errors and abuse. It is not used to identify you personally.

Browser storage

We store one small preference in your browser's local storage: how many deals per row you have chosen to view. It stays on your device, is readable only by this site, and is never transmitted to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

Third parties

Product images are loaded directly from Amazon's, Flipkart's and Myntra's own image servers rather than copied onto ours. As a result, your browser makes a request to those servers when a page loads, and they may log it under their own privacy policies.

When you click through to a deal, you leave DealMint. Anything you do on the seller's site — including any affiliate tracking on the link — is governed by that seller's privacy policy, not this one.

Your choices and contact

Because we hold no account data, there is generally no personal profile of you for us to export or delete. If you believe we hold information about you and want it removed, write to us and we will look into it.
