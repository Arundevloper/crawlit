About DealMint

DealMint finds genuinely discounted products across Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra, and keeps the list fresh automatically.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

What DealMint does

Online marketplaces list millions of products, and a real discount is easy to miss among them. DealMint watches those listings continuously and surfaces only the ones with a meaningful price cut — currently anything above 30% off.

Every deal you see was read directly from the seller's own public product page: the selling price, the maximum retail price, the discount, the rating and the product image.

How it works

  • Continuous crawling. Dozens of product categories are checked on a rolling schedule throughout the day, alongside the marketplace's own deals pages.
  • Price history. When a product's price changes, that change is recorded. This is what lets a deal be labelled as a price drop or as the lowest we have seen.
  • Freshness. Deals are ordered by when they were discovered, newest first, and anything not seen for a week drops off the list.
  • Filtering. Duplicate colour and size variants of the same product are collapsed to one entry, and unbranded filler listings are excluded from categories where they dominate.

What DealMint is not

DealMint is not a shop. We do not sell anything, hold stock, process payments, or handle orders, delivery, returns or warranties. Every purchase happens on Amazon or Flipkart under their terms, and your relationship as a buyer is with them.

We also do not set the prices shown. They are read from the seller's page at the time of the crawl and can change at any moment — see our Disclaimer.

How we make money

Some links on DealMint are affiliate links. If you buy through one, the seller may pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you — you pay exactly the same price either way.

Commission never influences which deals appear or how they are ranked. Ordering is driven by discovery time and discount, both computed from the seller's own data. Full detail is in our affiliate disclosure.
